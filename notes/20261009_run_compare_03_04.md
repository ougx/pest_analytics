## 2. Fit: the φ drop is the reweighting

`02_fit_same_weights.py`. Best iterations: 03 iteration 5, 04 iteration 6 (lowest measurement φ with each run's own weights).

| Both runs scored with | φ 03 | φ 04 | change |
|---|---|---|---|
| each run's own weights (what PEST reports) | 1.689e6 | 6.737e5 | −60.1% |
| 04's weights | 6.540e5 | 6.737e5 | +3.0% |
| 03's weights | 1.689e6 | 1.856e6 | +9.9% |
| weight_orig (obs.csv) | 7.831e5 | 8.509e5 | +8.7% |

- With the same weights, **04 fits slightly worse than 03** whichever weights are used; the 60% drop PEST reports is
  the lower weights, not a better fit.
- RMSE (measured units, observations weighted in both runs): head 16.82 → 16.91, flow 295.8 → 299.7.
- By site (`grpSite`, 128 sites weighted in both): RMSE better in 04 at 56, worse at 72. Largest increases:
  nc-76 42.3 → 67.1, t190mw-8, nc-43, nc-98, t168mw-6 (+6 to +8). Largest decreases: nc-228 61.5 → 29.0,
  nc-182 45.7 → 26.7, t132mw-1, nc-229, nc-230.

## 3. Parameters

`03_parameter_changes.py`. Best iterations, 132 adjustable parameters.

- 59 differ by more than a factor of 2 (hk 25, ss 13, vk 10, sfrk 7, sy 4). Largest: kmaxcr02 3668 → 44,
  sfrvk130 0.74 → 0.010, aniscr07 2.1 → 59, kmincr05 0.93 → 21.
- On a bound at the best iteration: none in 03; 7 in 04 (kmincr13, kmincr15, kminfn01, kminfn04, kminfn05, kminfn09, aniscr01).
- **In 04, kminfn01–10 start on their lower bound**: their initial value 0.1 equals the new lower bound (it was 1e-4 in 03).

## Caveats

- Observations and parameters are matched by name; a pilot point that moved but kept its name counts as the same parameter.
- "Best iteration" uses each run's own weights, as PEST does.
- Corrections to numbers given in chat on 2026-10-09: there are 183 weights at ×0.3 at two wells (nc-216s and nc-217d),
  not one observation; "before 2007" is exact for the weighted observations.
